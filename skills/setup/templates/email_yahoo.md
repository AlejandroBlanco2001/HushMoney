📧 **Yahoo Mail setup — 2 steps**

**Step 1: Enable IMAP access**
Yahoo Mail has IMAP enabled by default — nothing to do here.

**Step 2: Create an App Password**
Yahoo blocks regular passwords for IMAP. You need a dedicated App Password.

1. Go to: login.yahoo.com/account/security
2. Scroll to **App passwords** and click **Generate app password**
3. Select *Other app*, type `hushmoney`, click **Get app password**
4. Copy the password shown

Once you have it, come back here and I'll ask for your Yahoo address and that password.
