📧 **Gmail setup — 2 steps**

**Step 1: Enable IMAP**
1. Open Gmail → ⚙️ Settings → *See all settings*
2. Go to the **Forwarding and POP/IMAP** tab
3. Under *IMAP Access*, select **Enable IMAP**
4. Click *Save Changes*

**Step 2: Create an App Password**
Google blocks regular passwords for IMAP. You need a dedicated App Password.

1. Go to: myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
   *(You must have 2-Step Verification enabled — Google will prompt you if not)*
2. In the *App name* field type `hushmoney`
3. Click **Create**
4. Copy the 16-character password shown (no spaces)

Once you have it, come back here and I'll ask for your email address and that password.
