How it works

Four steps. Zero manual entry.

Your bank already sends a notification for every transaction. HushMoney turns that stream into a clean, categorized ledger — without ever touching your account.

01

Notification arrives

A bank SMS, push, or email lands — picked up over IMAP or a Telegram listener.

02

Redaction firewall

OTPs, account numbers and names are stripped. Only amount, merchant, date, type continue.

03

Agent reasons

Hermes categorizes the transaction, stores it, and spots recurring charges.

04

You get a summary

Daily, weekly and monthly rollups delivered to your own Telegram bot.

The privacy boundary

The AI never sees your raw bank data.

Redaction isn't a setting — it's the shape of the system. Everything downstream of the forwarder is untrusted by design.

  • Stripped at the edgeOTPs, account numbers and recipient names are removed in the forwarder, before anything reaches the model.
  • Learns from redacted text onlyWhen a new bank format appears, the model infers structure from already-redacted messages — word order and delimiters, never real values.
  • Self-hosted, single-userYou own the bot token, the database, and the box it runs on. The SQLite store never leaves your machine.
  • Sandboxed agentHermes runs inside an NVIDIA OpenShell policy that limits which files and endpoints it can reach.
