---
title: Summaries
description: Daily, weekly, and monthly spending rollups delivered to Telegram.
---

The summaries skill generates spending breakdowns on demand or on a schedule, delivered directly to your Telegram.

## On-demand summaries

Ask Hermes at any time:

```
summarize this week
summarize October
what did I spend last month?
give me a breakdown for September
```

## Scheduled summaries

Configure automatic delivery in `config/hermes.yaml`:

```yaml
summaries:
  daily:
    enabled: true
  weekly:
    enabled: true
    day: monday       # delivered every Monday morning
  monthly:
    enabled: true
    day: 1            # delivered on the 1st of each month
```

## What a summary includes

A typical weekly summary covers:

- **Total spending** for the period
- **Breakdown by category** — food, transport, entertainment, etc.
- **Top merchants** — where most money went
- **Notable transactions** — anything unusually large
- **Recurring charges** detected that week
- **Active debts** reminder (if any)

## Cash operation reminders

When a cash withdrawal is detected, Hermes sends an immediate prompt:

```
Cash withdrawal of $200,000 detected. Add a description? (e.g., groceries, taxi)
```

Reply with a short description and Hermes updates the transaction record. This is the one case where Hermes proactively contacts you outside of a summary.
