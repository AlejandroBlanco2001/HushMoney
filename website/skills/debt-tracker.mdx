---
title: Debt Tracker
description: Track money owed in both directions via natural language.
---

Tell Hermes about informal debts in plain language. It keeps a running list of who owes you and who you owe, across any currency.

## Recording a debt

```
Carlos owes me $50,000 from lunch
I owe María $120,000 — she paid for my concert ticket
Sofía borrowed $200,000 last weekend
```

## Settling a debt

```
Carlos paid me back
I settled the $120,000 with María
mark the Sofía debt as paid
```

When a debt is settled via a tracked bank transfer, Hermes links the settlement to the transaction automatically.

## Checking balances

```
who owes me money?
what do I owe?
show all active debts
```

A typical response:

```
Active debts:

You are owed:
• Carlos — $50,000 COP (lunch, Oct 14)
• Sofía — $200,000 COP (weekend loan, Oct 5)

You owe:
• María — $120,000 COP (concert ticket, Sep 28)

Net: you are owed $130,000 COP
```

## Notes

- Debts support any currency — just mention it: *"he owes me $35 USD"*
- There is no payment reminder schedule — debts are purely on-demand
- Settled debts are kept in the database for history but excluded from active balance queries
